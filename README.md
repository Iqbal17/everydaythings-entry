Two lines explaining rain to a 10-year-old: a sky-sized sponge, and the real reason the rain falls — a cloud is a crowd of tiny drops that just get too heavy.

> Rain is the sky wringing out a giant sponge, squeezing drops onto the ground until there is nothing left to squeeze.
> The sponge part works because clouds really do hold water and let it go, but a cloud is not one solid thing you can squeeze: it is a crowd of tiny drops that just get too heavy and fall.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
