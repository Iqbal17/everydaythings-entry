Two lines explaining rain to a 10-year-old: a sky-sized sponge, and an honest account of where the analogy holds and where it breaks.

> Rain is the sky wringing out a giant sponge, squeezing drops over the roofs until the sponge is light enough to float away.
> The sponge part works because clouds really do hold water and let it go, but a real sponge fits in your hand, and the sky never wrings anything out on purpose.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
